## Myself

While machines are powerful and seem intelligent in many ways, they do not posess either the true spark of life or the interesting randomness inherent to the organic process of human creativity.

I give honor to my Creator who I believe gave me wisdom and help at every step.

With this help, the ideas behind this program, including the specific mechanisms for information retrieval, are my own. They were not suggested by AI, but came to me in my own thinking, as I reflected about the challenges faced by educators, and potential solutions and resources I knew were available.

In this category is included:

- the overall architecture of information retrieval and storage utilizing Python, Playwright, SQL
- distance and commute time retrieval and storage, and related filter and sort results
- roadmap to potential expansion to future features including displaying school demographic/staffing data, and using data to predict trends of growing, declining, or stagnant enrollment
- the name of the tool
- the marketing phrases

These are of the invention of myself, or in the case of marketing phrases and graphic design, my wife Jessica and I.

I additionally have attempted at each step to try to code as much of this software by hand as possible, learning from the many resources and examples of fellow humans. While some code and examples are written by Claude, I try to keep this to a minimum, and to gain a certain level of mastery of new code concepts at each step before continuing to the next step. As I always remind my students, mistakes are a crucial part of the learning process.
